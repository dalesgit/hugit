Yesterday a small group of us met in the church library. The intent of the group is to meet on a regular basis and to read poetry together after the model of **lectio divina**. This is a practice that is very old and aims to listen to a text, closely and over several repetitions, paying attention to a circle of voices ranging from the words in front of us, the godly voice of the universe, and the voice of our conscience sending us into action or *metanoia*.

Well, that's a mouthful. We didn't really quite get there yesterday. There had been a death in the parish. It was the day after Ascension Day, and that day begins with Jesus's disciples experiencing **grief upon grief** as their Lord has passed out of their immediate sight. We couldn't help talking about deep matters.
